# Surface brief: index.html (portfolio, single page)

## Scope and mode

Experience. One static page (index.html + styles.css + app.js + content.js), English and Thai switchable. Visitor: a hiring manager or a prospective consultancy client, arriving from the resume or LinkedIn, skimming for under two minutes. Primary actions: email, download the resume PDF, message on LinkedIn.

## Audience, proof, constraints

- Content comes only from the resume (new_resume (9).pdf). Phone number is not shown. Client names as on the resume are shown.
- No screenshots of client systems exist. The hero demonstration is an authored, labelled-synthetic record, never presented as real data.
- Bilingual is a grammar, not a toggle afterthought: every record header carries both languages; the toggle swaps which leads.
- Rejected by the user: template/AI look, stiff corporate with no person in it, unequal Thai/English quality.

## Unresolved

- Deploy target open (static host). Resume PDF copied into the site as an asset.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is one business document that posts. Every project is a numbered record linked to the records it led to, so a career reads as a connected system, not a card grid. It refuses the hero + project cards + skill-icon arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt field (#0a5fc4 family) for the header band and hero; sheet #f2f4f7 with 1px #c4ccd6 hard rules like an ALV grid; ink #0b1624; status lights only in posted green and in-process amber. Dense ruled rows with column heads (No., Record, Type, Facts, Stack, Status), tree connectors with depth numbering (1, 1.1, 1.1.2), tabular numerals, a green POSTED rubber stamp as the single tilted element. Barlow Semi Condensed for Latin, Sarabun as the Thai voice, Martian Mono only for record keys and measured figures.

STORY: The visitor watches one synthetic delivery record post through receiving, picking, reconciliation, labelling, payment, and support, and understands within seconds that one engineer covers the whole chain. They then open records to read the resume evidence and finish on three ways to reach him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440x900. A 56px system bar: wordmark left, EN/TH toggle, Email and Resume PDF right. A cobalt field fills the upper two thirds: the name in condensed display type (6rem max) white on cobalt, a twin-language role line beneath, two hard-edged action buttons, and on the right the portrait in a 4:5 attachment frame with a POSTED stamp at about 3.5 degrees. The lower third is the sheet-coloured flow strip: six record cells (Receive, Pick, Reconcile, Label, Pay, Support) joined by a flow line, a status cursor posting them one by one on a single clock, captioned as a synthetic demo record.

FORM: Document flow / ALV grid (candidate 4 of the ordered list; seed key 850a5808). Raised by the OSPAAAL twin-language frame, the rebel menu's single deliberate off-axis element, the dive profile's single vertical axis with depth numbering, and the step row's single clock.
